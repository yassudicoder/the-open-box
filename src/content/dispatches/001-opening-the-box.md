There's a particular feeling when a tool just works. You install it, and it's
already doing the thing — no account screen, no onboarding tour, no "connect your
data" handshake with a server you've never met. It works **out of the box**.

That's the whole idea here. **The Open Box** is one maker shipping small, honest
tools that run on your machine.

## Two words, taken literally

**Open** means nothing is hidden. The tools are local-first: what they compute,
they compute on your device, and it stays there. No accounts. No servers. Zero
bytes uploaded unless _you_ are the one handing something off, deliberately.

**Box** means it works out of the box. Install, and go. The keyboard is the
primary interface. Speed is the point.

## The series so far

- **VOL.01 — Canned Responses.** Insert any saved reply at your cursor in one
  keystroke. Live in the Chrome Web Store.
- **VOL.02 — Continue AI.** Move an AI chat to another model without losing
  context. Submitted.
- **VOL.03 —** in research. More when it's real.

Everything ships in public, lands in the changelog, and shows up on the ledger.
The ledger is the receipt: tools shipped, zero servers, zero bytes uploaded.

Open the box. Take out a tool. Nothing leaves with it.
