**Continue AI** is in the Chrome Web Store review queue.

The problem it fixes is small and annoying: you're deep in a conversation with
one AI, you want a second opinion from another, and you'd have to copy-paste the
whole thread by hand — losing structure, losing context, losing the thread.

Continue AI packages the conversation and hands it off in **one click**. Works
across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Like everything here, it's local-first: the
handoff happens through your clipboard, on your machine. No account. Nothing
uploaded.

```
SELECT DESTINATION  ▸  CLAUDE / CHATGPT / GEMINI
TRANSFER            ▸  packages full context
RESULT              ▸  continue exactly where you left off
```

Status: **SUBMITTED**. It'll flip to **LIVE** on the ledger the moment it clears
review.
