The first volume out of the box: **Canned Responses & Email Templates**.

You write the same replies over and over. This makes them a keystroke. Hit
`ALT+A`, pick a template, and it drops in **at your cursor** — in Gmail, in
LinkedIn — with variables filled and abbreviations expanded.

- `;intro` + `Tab` expands an abbreviation inline
- `{ first_name }` and friends fill from context
- 7 languages
- **0 bytes uploaded** — your templates never leave your device

Status on the ledger: **LIVE**. This is the shape of everything that follows —
one small job, done fast, done locally, done out of the box.
